# El Bigote Design Language

## Brand Identity

El Bigote is a lively Mexican street food and cocktail venue that combines authentic flavours, colourful personality, music, and a welcoming atmosphere in Basingstoke.

The digital experience should communicate the feeling of visiting El Bigote: welcoming, energetic, social, and memorable.

The design should balance playful Mexican influences with a modern, premium feel.

Key words:

- Confident
- Authentic
- Vibrant
- Handmade
- Social
- Creative
- Lively
- Local
- Handcrafted

---

## Brand Personality

El Bigote should feel like a place that welcomes people in, rather than simply a restaurant that serves food.

The brand personality should be:

- Warm
- Friendly
- Passionate
- Playful
- Authentic
- Confident

The website should feel like an invitation into the venue.

It should avoid feeling corporate or overly polished, while still communicating quality.

---

## Brand Scale

El Bigote should feel like a successful local venue with the foundations of a growing brand.

The website should prioritise the current Basingstoke experience while allowing space for future growth through:

- Events
- Live music
- Private bookings
- Collaborations
- Future locations

---

## Target Audience

### Local Diners

People in Basingstoke looking for:

- Somewhere different to eat
- A casual meal
- A night out
- A lively atmosphere

### Food Enthusiasts

People attracted by:

- Authentic Mexican flavours
- Street food culture
- Quality ingredients
- Unique cocktails

### Social Visitors

People attracted by:

- A good atmosphere
- A memorable experience
- Music and events
- Somewhere to spend time with friends

---

## Website Goals

The website should help visitors quickly answer:

### What is this place?

Authentic Mexican street food and cocktails in Basingstoke.

### Why should I go?

Great food, drinks, atmosphere, music, and a memorable experience.

### How do I visit?

Provide clear information about:

- Location
- Opening times
- Menu
- Booking/order information
- Events

---

## Tone of Voice

The El Bigote voice should be:

- Friendly and welcoming
- Authentic and passionate
- Playful without being gimmicky
- Confident without being corporate

The tone should combine the energy of a local favourite with the authenticity of Mexican street food culture.

---

## Digital Experience Principles

The website should feel like stepping into El Bigote.

Visitors should immediately experience:

- The atmosphere of the venue
- The personality of the brand
- The quality of the food and drinks
- The feeling of wanting to visit

The website should prioritise:

- Visual storytelling
- Simple navigation
- Mobile experience
- Fast access to important information

The website should not just display information, it should create excitement about visiting.

---

## Visual Inspiration

The website should take inspiration from the physical venue rather than recreate it. 

The building artwork, colours, logo, food, and atmosphere should influence the digital experience.

The website should not copy the walls or artwork directly. Instead:

- Mural colours &rarr; Accent palette
- Logo &rarr; Brand recognition
- Food photography &rarr; Visual focus
- Venue atmosphere &rarr; Emotional tone

---

## Logo Usage

The El Bigote logo should be used as a recognisable brand anchor rather than a repeated decoration.

The logo should appear prominently in key locations, especially the navigation/header.

The logo should not overwhelm the experience.

The website should allow the photography, typography, and atmosphere to communicate the wider brand.

---

## Design Pillars

### Authenticity

The website should feel connected to the real venue.

Prioritise:

- Real photography
- Real stories
- Real atmosphere

Avoid:

- Stock imagery
- Generic restaurant language

### Energy

El Bigote is not a quiet fine dining restaurant.

The website should communicate:

- Music
- Social occasions
- Celebration
- Personality

### Craft

The website should showcase:

- Quality food
- Cocktails
- Attention to detail
- Handmade elements

### Growth

The website should support the current venue while creating a foundation for future opportunities.

---

## Colour Palette

The site should not be overwhelmed with colour.

The physical venue already provides strong colour through artwork and atmosphere.

The website should use colour intentionally.

### Base Colours

- Warm cream (backgrounds, sections, cards)
- Deep charcoal (text, dark sections, contrast)

### Accent Colours

- Terracotta (brick, clay, warm Mexican tones)
- Agave green (plants, natural materials)
- Golden yellow (food, sunlight, murals)

---

## Typography

Typography should balance personality and readability.

### Headings

Headings should carry personality and confidence.

Potential styles:

- Bebas Neue
- Oswald
- Anton
- Archivo Black

### Body

Body text should prioritise readability.

Potential styles:

- Inter
- Manrope
- Geist

---

## Photography Style

Photography should focus on:

- Food quality
- Cocktails
- People enjoying the venue
- Atmosphere
- Real moments

Images should feel:

- Warm
- Vibrant
- Authentic
- Social

---

## Layout Principles

### Large Photography

Food and atmosphere are important, not excessive text.

### Generous Spacing

Avoid clutter.

Allow colours, photography, and content to breathe.

### Strong Hierarchy

A visitor should immediately understand:

- What is El Bigote?
- What food do they serve?
- Where are they?
- How do I visit?

---

## UI Style

### Buttons

- Slightly rounded corners
- Bold typography
- Strong contrast

### Cards

- Subtle shadows
- Warm backgrounds
- Minimal borders

### Animations

- Minimal
- Slow
- Subtle
- Purposeful

Animations should enhance the experience, not distract from it.

---

## Things To Avoid

- Generic restaurant templates
- Stock Mexican imagery
- Overuse of sombreros/cacti/skulls
- Making everything bright and colourful
- Heavy animation
- Making the website feel like a takeaway menu
- Corporate or overly formal language
